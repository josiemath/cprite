Getting started

Check your device

CPRite runs on iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later. It needs the phone's built-in motion sensors, which every supported iPhone has.

Attach your phone

Secure the iPhone to the back of your top hand, screen facing up, so it can't slide. Open Placement Guide on the home screen for step-by-step help.

Set up a session

Tap Start Practice, choose Coach or Assessment mode, patient type and number of rescuers, then tap Begin.

Practice on a manikin

After the 3-2-1 countdown, start compressions. Tap Finish & See Results when you're done.

Frequently asked questions

No compressions are being counted. What should I check?
  • Make sure the phone is firmly attached to the back of your hand, screen facing up and lying flat.
  • Wait for the 3-2-1 countdown to finish. The first second after it ends is ignored while the sensor settles.
  • Use firm, straight up-and-down compressions. Side-to-side or rocking motion is filtered out.
  • Open Sensor Lab on the home screen to check that live motion values change as you move.
Why does my pace jump around?

The pace dial updates with each compression and averages your last few. Uneven rhythm, pauses or a loosely attached phone make it jump. Try the 110 BPM metronome in Setup to lock in a steady beat.

Does CPRite measure compression depth?

Not yet. CPRite measures compression rate, rhythm, count and timing. It does not measure depth, chest recoil or whether breaths are delivered. Use a feedback manikin or an instructor to check depth.

I can't hear voice prompts or the metronome.

Turn up your iPhone's media volume and make sure Voice Prompts or Pace Metronome is turned on in Setup. Voice prompts and the metronome are only available in Coach mode.

What's the difference between Coach and Assessment mode?

Coach mode gives live pace feedback, breath prompts, haptics and compressor-switch reminders. Assessment mode hides live coaching and shows your full report when you finish, so you can test yourself.

How is my score calculated?

Your 0–100 score combines the share of compressions in the 100–120 per minute target zone (60%), how steady your rhythm was (25%), and how much of the session you spent compressing (15%).

Can I use CPRite in a real emergency?

No. CPRite is a practice and training aid only, and it is not a medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US) right away and follow the dispatcher's instructions.

Where is my practice history stored, and how do I delete it?

Your sessions are stored only on your iPhone. To delete one, open Progress and swipe left on it. To delete everything, delete the CPRite app. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does CPRite work in landscape?

Yes. The practice screen switches to a landscape layout automatically when you rotate your phone. If it doesn't rotate, check that Portrait Orientation Lock is off in Control Center.

Medical disclaimer: CPRite is for CPR training and practice only. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not replace certified CPR training. Always practice on a training manikin, never on a person.
