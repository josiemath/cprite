Everything you need to practice well

Built for students, first-aid learners and anyone who wants their CPR rhythm to become second nature.

⏱️

Live pace dial

A dial shows your compressions per minute as you go, with the 100–120 target zone in green and clear "speed up" or "slow down" cues.

🫁

Breath and cycle prompts

CPRite counts each cycle and tells you when to give breaths, using 30:2 for adults and 15:2 for children with two or more rescuers.

👥

Team CPR timing

Practicing with a partner? You'll get a reminder every two minutes to switch compressors with minimal interruption.

🔊

Voice, haptics and metronome

Hear spoken cues, feel vibrations at key moments, or follow a steady 110 BPM beat. Turn each one on or off to suit you.

📈

Scores and progress

See your score, time in the target zone, rhythm steadiness and a pace chart after each session, then track your trend over time.

✅

Assessment mode

Hide the live coaching and test yourself. Your full performance report appears when you finish.

How it works

Set up in under a minute.

Attach your phone

Secure your iPhone to the back of your top hand, screen facing up. The in-app Placement Guide shows you how.

Choose your session

Pick Coach or Assessment mode, adult or child/infant, and one or two-plus rescuers.

Start compressing

After a 3-2-1 countdown, CPRite counts each compression and coaches your pace, breaths and cycles.

Review your results

Get your score and pace chart, and every session is saved so you can watch yourself improve.

🔒 Private by design

CPRite has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your motion data is processed on your iPhone, and your practice history stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
