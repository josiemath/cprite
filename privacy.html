Privacy Policy

The short version: CPRite does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

Effective date: November 10, 2026

🔒 At a glance

No accounts. No sign-in. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No third-party SDKs. CPRite does not send any data to us or to anyone else. Everything the app does happens on your iPhone.

Who we are

This policy explains how the CPRite iOS app ("CPRite", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. CPRite is a CPR practice and training aid for iPhone, developed and published by Jocelyn Mathew.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. CPRite has no user accounts, makes no network requests for your data, and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services from third parties.

Data the app uses on your device

To work, CPRite uses some information locally on your iPhone. None of it leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

DataWhy it is usedWhere it stays
Motion sensor readings (accelerometer)To detect and count chest compressions and measure your pace during practice.Processed in real time on your iPhone. Raw readings are not saved.
Practice session results (date, mode, compression count, pace, score and similar)To show your results and your progress over time.Stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone.
Sensor Lab recordings (optional)Lets you export motion readings as a CSV file for your own review.Created on your iPhone. Shared only if you choose to send it using the iOS share sheet.
Voice promptsSpoken coaching cues during practice.Generated on your iPhone using Apple's built-in speech. No audio is recorded.

CPRite does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, health data or any other personal information.

How to delete your data

  • To delete a single session, open Progress in the app and swipe left on it.
  • To delete all CPRite data, delete the app from your iPhone. This removes all stored sessions.

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information, because we do not collect any. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple may include CPRite's stored practice sessions in that backup. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Children's privacy

CPRite does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app can be used without providing any personal information.

Medical disclaimer

CPRite is for CPR training and practice only. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and must not be used in a real emergency. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US) immediately.

Changes to this policy

If we change how CPRite handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change means the app starts collecting data, we will tell you in the app before it happens.
